For distance racers, iPhone and iPad

Your rivals, read from their AIS

The boats you are racing don't need to run anything. If they carry an AIS transponder, AIS Sail Race works out, live, what each one is doing and what it means for you.

Crossings

The next meeting with each boat around you: in 3:40, 120 m apart, which boat passes ahead, and when it's close, who keeps clear: port gives way to starboard, windward to leeward.

Tacks

Each boat's tack from its course over ground against the true wind, how long it has held it, every tack and gybe on a timeline, and an alert when a pinned rival tacks.

Gains & leverage

Speed, pointing and VMG against yours, miles per hour gained or lost, and what a 10° shift either way would do to the gap.

The fleet's wind

Boats beating on both tacks straddle the wind. The app finds the bisector, follows it, calls each shift and says which tack it lifts.

Night vision

One switch turns the whole screen red, so a glance at the fleet on a night watch doesn't cost you your night sight.

Offshore

Out of range of shore stations, join the boat's Wi-Fi and read AIS, GPS and true wind straight from the chartplotter or transponder. No internet needed.

Near the coast, boats arrive from AIS stations ashore (AISStream.io) through the app's relay. Forecast wind by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). A demo race with fictional boats shows every screen when nobody is around.
