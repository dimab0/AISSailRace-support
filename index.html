Read the fleet around you.

AIS Sail Race turns the AIS reports of nearby sailboats into race intelligence for distance racers: speeds, tacks, time on each tack, and who is gaining on you.

What it shows

  • Average speeds of every nearby sailboat: now, over 10 minutes, the last hour and since tracking began.
  • Each boat's tack — port or starboard — read from its course over ground against the wind, with time on the current tack, tack and gybe counts and a timeline.
  • Versus you: speed, pointing or depth, VMG, where they are relative to you upwind, and how fast they're gaining or losing.
  • The fleet's wind: the wind direction the fleet's own tacking angles show, and wind shifts as they happen.
  • A mark: rank the fleet by distance and speed made good to a mark you place.
  • Tack alerts when a boat in your fleet tacks or gybes.

Where the data comes from

Near the coast, boats heard by AIS receivers ashore (AISStream.io). Offshore, connect the app to your chartplotter, AIS transponder or NMEA Wi-Fi gateway: it reads AIS targets, your GPS and true wind directly, with no internet. Forecast wind by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
