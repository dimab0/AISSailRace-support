Privacy policy · effective October 19, 2026

Nothing about you leaves the boat

In one line

AIS Sail Race collects no personal data: there are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your location

With your permission, and only while the app is open, it uses your position to choose which area's AIS reports to receive and to compare other boats with yours. To fetch boats and forecast wind it sends the app's relay server the edges of the area to watch (a box around you) and a rounded position for the forecast. The relay passes these to AISStream.io and Open-Meteo.com and keeps no record of them: it stores nothing and logs nothing.

Your boat's network

If you turn on the boat network, the app connects to the address you enter on the boat's Wi-Fi to read NMEA data. That data stays on your device.

Stored on your device

Your settings, pinned rivals, your mark, and the recent tracks of boats around you (up to 12 hours), so a relaunch mid-race keeps the picture. Remove them with Setup → Clear tracks, or by deleting the app.

Other boats

The app shows AIS reports, which vessels broadcast openly by radio for collision avoidance.

Third parties

AIS data: AISStream.io. Forecast wind: Open-Meteo.com. Both receive requests from the relay, never from your device.

Children, changes, contact

The app collects personal information from no one, children included. A changed policy will appear here with a new date. Questions: braznichenko@gmail.com.
