Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

The short version

AIS Sail Race does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your location

With your permission the app uses your position, while it is open, to choose which area's AIS reports to show and to compare other boats with yours. To fetch the boats and the forecast wind, the app sends our relay server the edges of the area to watch (a box around your position) and a rounded position for the forecast. The relay passes these on to AISStream.io and Open-Meteo.com to get the data, and keeps no record of them: it stores nothing and logs nothing.

Your boat's network

If you turn on the boat network, the app connects to the address you enter on your boat's Wi-Fi to read NMEA data. That data stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your settings, the boats you pinned, your mark, and the recent tracks of the boats around you (up to 12 hours), so a relaunch mid-race keeps the picture. Clear them with Settings → Clear tracks, or delete the app.

Other boats

The app shows AIS reports, which vessels broadcast publicly by radio for collision avoidance.

Third parties

AIS data: AISStream.io. Forecast wind: Open-Meteo.com. They receive requests from our relay, not from your device.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
